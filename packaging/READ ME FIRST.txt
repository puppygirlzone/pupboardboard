pupboard
========

A colourful homepage that shows your gaming, music, films/TV and news at a glance.


STARTING IT

  Windows:  double-click "Start pupboard.bat".
            If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info",
            then "Run anyway". (It says this about any new download that
            isn't from a big company.)

  macOS:    right-click "Start pupboard.command" and choose Open, then Open
            again. (Only needed the first time; after that, double-click.)

  Linux:    double-click "start-pupboard.sh" and choose "Run in terminal",
            or run ./start-pupboard.sh in a terminal.

A small black window opens, then your browser opens pupboard. Keep the black
window open while you use the dashboard. Closing it stops pupboard.


SETTING IT UP

The first time, your browser opens the Settings page. Fill in what you use
and press Save. Anything you skip just shows "Not set up".

  - Game deals and gaming news work straight away, nothing to fill in.
  - Spotify: the easy way is a free ListenBrainz account (listenbrainz.org).
    Connect Spotify there, then type your ListenBrainz username in Settings.
  - Letterboxd: just your username.
  - Steam: paste your profile link, plus a free key from
    steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey (the Settings page explains).

To change settings later, start pupboard and click the gear button at the
top right of the dashboard. For safety, the settings page only works in a
browser on this same computer, using the link shown in the black window.


OPENING IT ON YOUR PHONE

On the Settings page, under Network, choose "Any device on my home network",
save, and restart pupboard. Then open http://<this computer's IP>:8742 on
your phone. (Your firewall may ask whether to allow it: allow it on private
networks only.)


UPDATING

Your settings, feeds and gaming history live in the "data" and "config"
folders. A new version never replaces them, so updating keeps everything.

  1. Close the black pupboard window.
  2. Windows: right-click the new zip, choose "Extract All...", pick the
     folder that contains your current "pupboard" folder (not the pupboard
     folder itself), and choose "Replace the files in the destination".
     Linux: unzip -o the new zip in the same place as before.
     macOS: unzipping makes a new "pupboard 2" folder instead. Copy the
     "data" and "config" folders from your old pupboard folder into it,
     then use the new one.
  3. Start pupboard again.


STOPPING / REMOVING

Close the black window to stop it. To remove pupboard, delete this folder.
Nothing is installed anywhere else.
